Conclusion générale 
 
This project has laid the foundation of a web centralized system for rainfall data management in 
Tunisia. By replacing a manual and error-prone process with a  web application built around a postgres  
database, we have demonstrated the feasibility and relevance of an integrated approach.  
The current solution meets the immediate needs for data collection, centralization, and consultation, 
representing a significant step forward for the General Directorate for Water Resources (DGRE) and 
its partners. 
 
However, this work is only the first step toward a deeper digital transformation. The future work is 
extensive and revolves around several strategic axes to evolve this tool into a national kowledge 
managament systemfor water ressources  
 
The next step will be to deploy the application in a controlled test environment with end-users (CRDA 
agents, ministry staff). This phase will allow for the collection of  user feedback  to identify bugs, 
optimize usability, and validate the relevance of the features. A continuous improvement cycle based 
on this feedback will be established to refine the application. 
 
Once the application is stable, the objective will be to make it accessible to a wider audience. This will 
involve concrete technical steps: 
• Hosting on a physical server : To ensure performance, security, and data sovereignty, the 
application will be hosted on a dedicated server within the ministry's infrastructure. 
• Purchasing a domain name  (www.pluviometrie.tn) will be acquired to facilitate access to the 
platform. 
• The application will be officially launched and made accessible to the general public, including 
farmers, researchers, the media, and citizens interested in water data. 
 
the long -term vision is to transcend simple data management to create a Knowledge Management 
System (KMS) dedicated to water resources. The PLUVIOM application will be the first module of 
this integrated platform. Future upgrades will aim to integrate other types of hydrological data: 
• Groundwater table levels.

• River flow rates. 
• Dam filling rates. 
• Water quality. 
 
This KMS will not only centralize information but also contextualize, analyze, and transform it into 
actionable knowledge. It will integrate predictive analysis tools, dynamic dashboards, and simulation 
models to support strategic decision -making in water management, thereby making a significant 
contribution to Tunisia's water security
